## Privacy Policy for Stack Decoded

**Last updated: 26 September 2026**

Stack Decoded ("we", "the channel") publishes educational videos on YouTube, Instagram and
Facebook. This policy explains what our own software does with data.

### What this software is

Stack Decoded is produced by software that runs on the channel owner's personal computer. It
writes and renders the videos, then uploads them to accounts the owner controls. It is not a
product, a service or an app that other people sign into. There are no users other than the
owner.

### What we collect from viewers

**Nothing.** We have no website that collects data, no sign-up, no newsletter, no analytics of
our own, no cookies, no advertising trackers, and no contact forms.

If you watch our videos, comment on them or subscribe, you are interacting with YouTube,
Instagram or Facebook, and that is covered by their privacy policies, not ours. We can see the
same public statistics and comments any channel owner can see on their own platform dashboard.

### What our software accesses

Our software uses the YouTube Data API and the Meta (Instagram and Facebook) APIs to publish
videos to our own accounts. To do so it holds credentials issued by Google and Meta that allow
it to upload and manage content on those accounts, and nothing else.

Specifically:

- It uploads video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to our own channel.
- It reads back the resulting video identifiers and public statistics for our own videos.
- It does **not** read, collect or store any information about viewers, subscribers or any
  other person's account.
- It does **not** request access to anyone else's Google, Instagram or Facebook account, and
  nobody else signs in to it.

### Where credentials are kept

The access credentials are stored in a single file on the owner's own computer. They are never
shared, never sent anywhere except to Google's and Meta's own servers when publishing, and
never included in the published source code. They can be revoked at any time from the Google
Account permissions page or the Meta account settings, which immediately stops the software
from publishing anything.

### Data retention

We keep no personal data, so there is nothing to retain, export or delete. The video files and
scripts we produce are kept on the owner's own computer.

### Children

Our videos are for people learning to program and are not directed at children. The channel is
marked "not made for kids" on YouTube.
